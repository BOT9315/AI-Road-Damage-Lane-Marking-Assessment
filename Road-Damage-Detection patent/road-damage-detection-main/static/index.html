Dual-Stream Deep Learning & Dynamic IPM for Pavement Intelligence

A real-time civil engineering system designed to bridge visual distress recognition with physical metric surface area computation (cm²), depth distance projection, contractor accountability tracking, and photometric lane marking serviceability.

Why This Technology is Necessary

Conventional road maintenance relies on manual ocular inspections or multi-million-dollar Laser Profilometer vans that cannot scale across rural and semi-urban road networks. Potholes and progressive fatigue fractures go undetected until catastrophic pavement failure occurs, causing vehicular accidents, severe traffic bottlenecks, and exponential municipal rehabilitation costs.

Why It Is Available for Patent

This invention combines three non-obvious engineering mechanisms:

  • Dual-Stream Hybrid Extraction: YOLO deep learning combined with dynamic multi-scale blackhat morphology for water-filled and reflective road cavities.
  • Real-Time Dynamic IPM Ground-Sampling: Converts 2D camera pixels directly to metric units (cm² and distance) based on calibrated pitch and focal length without physical depth sensors.
  • Corridor Identity & Contractor Liability Linkage: Bridges physical pavement distress metrics to municipal construction registries and defect liability periods.
